`ablift duration` and `ablift power` now accept `--expected-split` (and an `expected_split` field or column in duration `--input` files) to plan sample sizes, run duration, power, and MDE under unequal traffic allocations (such as 90/10 rollouts) across all methods and both `conversion_rate` and `arpu` metrics. Duration and power outputs now report `n_by_variant` and `n_total` alongside `n_per_variant`, and `ablift power` adds `--n-total` so unequal-split plans round-trip cleanly.
